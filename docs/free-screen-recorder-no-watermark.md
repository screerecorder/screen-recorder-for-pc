## Why Some Free Screen Recorders Add a Watermark

A watermark on a free screen recorder for PC means the software is a freemium product — the recording feature is free but the company earns money by branding your videos until you upgrade. Common examples include Bandicam (free version watermarks recordings), Camtasia (30-day trial only), and Movavi Screen Recorder (free version adds a watermark after 5 minutes).

## How to Check If a Screen Recorder Adds a Watermark

Before committing to any free screen recorder for PC:

1. Record a 30-second test clip.
2. Play it back in full and watch for a semi-transparent logo, brand name, or URL overlay — usually in a corner.
3. Export a 5-minute clip (some recorders watermark only after a time threshold).
4. Check the exported file, not just the in-app preview.

If a watermark appears on the exported file, the tool requires a paid upgrade to remove it.
